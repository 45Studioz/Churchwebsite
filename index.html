Our Legacy

A Century of Grace

Rooted in faith, built on prayer, and sustained by community — for 100 remarkable years.

Est. 1926

Built on Faith, Sustained by Grace

From humble beginnings to a beacon of hope in Thika, A.C.K. Thika Memorial Church has been a spiritual home for generations. Our centenary is a living testament to God's unwavering love and the dedication of our founders, members, and leaders.

  • Founded in 1926

    Proclaiming the Gospel for a full century in Thika.

  • Thousands of Services

    Worship, weddings, baptisms, and dedications over 100 years.

  • A Vibrant Community

    Serving Thika and beyond with love and compassion.

  • Part of A.C.K. Diocese of Thika

    Standing united in the Anglican Church of Kenya.

Voices of Praise

Lifting Hearts Through Song

From traditional hymns to contemporary anthems, the A.C.K. Thika Memorial Choir has been a living instrument of worship for decades. Their voices have graced countless services, weddings, funerals, and community events — bringing comfort, joy, and the presence of God to all who hear.
